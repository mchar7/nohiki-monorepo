# Senior Backend Engineer, Platform Team

## Company: CloudScale Technologies

Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)
Salary: $180,000 - $220,000

## About the Role

We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Platform Team and help build the next generation of our cloud-native infrastructure. You'll work on designing and implementing scalable microservices, event-driven architectures, and developer tooling that powers our core product.

## Responsibilities

- Design and implement scalable backend services using Python and Go
- Build and maintain event-driven architectures with Apache Kafka
- Develop and operate Kubernetes-based infrastructure
- Collaborate with ML engineers to deploy and serve machine learning models
- Establish best practices for observability, monitoring, and alerting
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to technical architecture decisions
- Drive improvements to our CI/CD pipeline and developer experience

## Requirements

- 5+ years of experience in backend software engineering
- Strong proficiency in Python and at least one systems language (Go, Rust, C++)
- Experience with distributed systems and microservices architectures
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and Docker
- Experience with message brokers (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar)
- Familiarity with SQL and NoSQL databases
- Strong understanding of software engineering best practices

## Nice to Have

- Experience with ML infrastructure and model serving
- Contributions to open-source projects
- Experience with GitOps and ArgoCD
- Familiarity with OpenTelemetry and observability tooling
- Experience with vector databases (Qdrant, Pinecone, Weaviate)
